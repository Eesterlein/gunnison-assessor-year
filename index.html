      Year-round: abatements

      An owner who thinks they were overtaxed for a past year (an error, illegal or erroneous valuation, or clerical mistake) can petition for abatement or refund within two years after taxes were due. Petitions go to the Assessor, then the Commissioners.

      Year-round: exemption changes

      A senior or veteran exemption stays in place until ownership or occupancy changes. Owners must report a change within 60 days. One exemption per home per year.

      Weekend and holiday rule

      When a deadline falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, the filing is timely on the next business day. The dates here are already shifted; open an item to see the statutory date.
